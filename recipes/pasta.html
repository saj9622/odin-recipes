Spaghetti Carbonara

A classic Roman pasta dish with creamy egg-based sauce.

Ingredients

  • 400g spaghetti
  • 200g pancetta
  • 3 large eggs
  • 1 cup grated Pecorino Romano
  • Freshly ground black pepper

Steps

  1. Cook spaghetti al dente
  2. Fry pancetta until crispy
  3. Mix eggs and cheese in a bowl
  4. Combine all ingredients while pasta is hot
