Greek Salad

Refreshing Mediterranean salad with feta cheese.

Ingredients

  • 3 ripe tomatoes
  • 1 cucumber
  • 1 red onion
  • 200g feta cheese
  • Kalamata olives
  • Olive oil

Steps

  1. Chop all vegetables
  2. Arrange in serving dish
  3. Top with feta and olives
  4. Dress with olive oil
